About:

Welcome to Maunga Club! We are a club that offers members the opportunity to stay in comfortable mountain chalets while enjoying the excitement of skiing and snowboarding. Our chalets provide a place to relax, spend time with family and friends, and enjoy the beauty of the mountains. Whether you are an experienced skier or new to the snow, Maunga Club is a great way to enjoy mountain adventures. Our goal is to provide members with a welcoming place to stay and create unforgettable winter memories. Join Maunga Club today and discover your next mountain getaway!


Kākāpo

The largest chalet, accommodating up to 10 adults and 30 children. Features a large kitchen, dining and living area, with great amenities.


Kererū

Perfect for families, with 2 bedrooms, a separate living area, and space for up to 2 adults and 4 children.


Pūkeko

Ideal for families and larger groups, sleeping up to 6 adults and 15 children. Enjoy a comfortable stay with your group in your own chalet.
