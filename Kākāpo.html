Kākāpo Chalet

Kākāpo Chalet information

Kākāpo is the largest chalet offered by Club Maunga, making it an excellent choice for large families, school groups, and friends looking for a mountain getaway. With accommodation for up to 10 adults and 30 children, it can host groups of up to 40 people. The chalet features a large kitchen, dining area, and living space, providing plenty of room for groups to prepare meals, eat together, and relax after a day on the slopes. Its great amenities help make it a convenient option for a comfortable group stay. Located on the mountain, Kākāpo offers the advantage of skiing to and from the chalet, giving guests easy access to the surrounding ski and snowboard terrain.
